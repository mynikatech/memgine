# Counter redemption savings: S2 investigation notes

These notes are a partial, read-only investigation. They do not authorize or implement realized-savings recording. Complete the remaining provider, refund, restoration, and exception audit before designing persistence.

## Existing integration points

`CounterCommercePaymentService` prepares and reviews redemption checkouts via `commerce_prepare_counter_redemption_checkout`, `commerce_review_counter_redemption_checkout`, and `commerce_quote_counter_redemption`. Its JDBI interface reads `commerce_get_counter_redemption_lines`, `commerce_get_counter_redemption_adjustments`, and the persisted pricing reconciliation. Product selection is handled by `select_counter_redemption_item_product_mapping`; checkout preparation and review were most recently replaced in migrations 281–282.

`commerce_begin_counter_redemption_cash_payment` starts a cash attempt and `commerce_confirm_counter_redemption_cash_payment` confirms it and runs the existing redemption finalizer. The service's `finalizeRemoteTerminalPayment` calls `commerce_finalize_counter_payment_by_reference` and handles financial exceptions and late callback reconciliation. Zero-value completion uses `commerce_complete_counter_redemption_zero_value`; `commerce_finalize_counter_redemption_transaction` is the existing redemption finalizer. `commerce_close_counter_redemption_unpaid` is a separate closure path. Timeout recovery is routed through `CounterTimeoutRecoverySql`; recovered terminal callbacks are retained as evidence and do not start another payment.

## Candidate design and unresolved questions

Actual savings should use finalized transaction line and adjustment evidence for the actual selected product and currency, not the advertised maximum. Investigate recording at the existing successful fulfillment boundary, with an idempotency key tied to the finalized transaction and adjustment identities. Do not record savings from pending, failed, cancelled, unpaid, or financially unresolved attempts. Refunds and restoration require auditable reversal or correction entries, and customer, membership, and business totals must be derived without double-counting retries or overlapping adjustments.

Before implementation, trace the precise terminal callback and late-capture paths, cash and zero-value finalization, refund completion, restoration, partial refund allocation, tax treatment, adjustment attribution, and financial-exception recovery. Confirm whether existing immutable receipts or transaction/adjustment records already provide sufficient per-item evidence and what constitutes final success for each path. Do not introduce a parallel ledger until those questions are resolved.

## Refund, restoration, and exception boundaries

- The terminal callback enters through PoyntPaymentBridgeCallbackRoutes and records provider detail through commerce_record_remote_payment_callback_detail. CounterCommercePaymentService.finalizeRemoteTerminalPayment calls commerce_finalize_counter_payment_by_reference. Investigate the committed commerce and redemption item states after the canonical finalizer, including zero-value and reconciled transactions, before recording savings.
- CounterTimeoutRecoverySql invokes commerce_counter_timeout_recover. A recovered attempt is recognized by commerce_counter_terminal_attempt_recovered. The late callback branch and commerce_close_counter_redemption_unpaid must not create savings merely because provider evidence was recorded or staff closed the checkout.
- CommerceFinancialExceptionSql opens a case through commerce_open_remote_terminal_financial_exception or commerce_open_counter_financial_exception_for_recovery. It reconciles unresolved terminal evidence through commerce_reconcile_unresolved_remote_terminal_financial_exception and retries fulfillment through commerce_recover_counter_financial_exception. Recovery should use the same finalized adjustment identity as ordinary completion.
- PaymentRefundService starts commerce refunds through payment_refund_request_commerce. Terminal bridge outcomes are resolved through payment_refund_record_poynt_bridge_callback or payment_refund_bridge_confirm_verified. Use a verified successful refund and its original transaction reference as reversal evidence. Keep refund requests, pending outcomes, and failed outcomes out of realized totals.
- RedemptionResolutionService uses redemption_resolution_request and redemption_resolution_complete for RESTORE_ONLY, REFUND_ONLY, and REFUND_AND_RESTORE. A restored entitlement and a refunded amount are separate facts. For a combined resolution, correlate both with the original item and make the accounting correction once.
- Keep the total attributed saving reconciled to finalized line adjustments. Open design questions include proportional or line-specific attribution for partial refunds, stacked discounts, tax-inclusive versus pre-tax reporting, and how to handle refund-only versus restore-only adjustments to reporting without changing either underlying workflow.

Use commerce_get_counter_redemption_lines and commerce_get_counter_redemption_adjustments to trace quantity, selected product, immutable price and applied adjustment evidence. First prove that stored finalized rows contain a stable link to the redemption item, subscription, benefit or offer, and that the adjustment amount represents the final allocated discount rather than its requested value. Exclude non-benefit/offer adjustments. Reconcile the sum to the corresponding finalized discount components and preserve the existing amount-due calculation. Zero-value completion can still have a positive applied discount.

Prefer a PostgreSQL projection from immutable finalized evidence. If the audit reveals missing immutable attribution, store only the missing evidence at the existing atomic fulfillment boundary, uniquely keyed by commerce transaction and adjustment. Never create a second entry when callback, cash confirmation, or recovery is replayed. Unresolved or late callback evidence alone does not qualify. Refund and restoration corrections must link to the original allocation and verified refund/resolution identities so retries cannot reverse the same value twice. Decide whether restoration alone affects lifetime financial savings, how partial refunds allocate across lines, and whether tax savings are reported separately before exposing net totals.
